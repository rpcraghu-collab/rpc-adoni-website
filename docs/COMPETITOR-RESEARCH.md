# RPC Adoni: competitor and UX research

Researched 2026-11-08 with web search and page fetches. No browser was used. Justdial, Adonionline, CADD Centre and Aptech blocked fetching (403 or connection refused), so their notes come from search snippets only.

## 1. Local competitors

**Main finding: nobody in Adoni has a good website.** Justdial lists about 30 computer institutes in Adoni, and none of them comes up in search with a real course website. The pages that rank for "Java course in Adoni" come from out-of-town SEO farms. Kurnool and Anantapur have a few weak sites. That leaves the region open for RPC.

## 3. Synthesis for RPC

### Top 10 patterns to copy

1. **Hero = local promise + 2 CTAs + proof strip.** For example: "Job-ready computer courses in Adoni since 2000", with buttons **"WhatsApp us"** (prefilled: "Hi RPC, I want details about ___ course") and **"Book a free demo class"**. Under them, a strip of real facts only: Since 2000 · ISO 9001:2015 · AP DET permitted (2006) · MSME registered · 5 Education Excellence awards.
2. **Sticky mobile bottom bar: Call · WhatsApp · Enquire** (Jetking). On desktop, a floating WhatsApp button. Most enquiries in Adoni will come from phones.
3. **Course cards with the 5 facts parents ask about:** badge (Diploma / Certificate / BTech add-on), duration, who it's for (eligibility), mode (classroom / lab), outcome line ("Prepare GST invoices in Tally"), and a fee line ("Fee: ₹[NEED]" or "Ask fee on WhatsApp"). Plus 2 buttons: "Details" and "WhatsApp about this course".
4. **Filter tabs on Courses:** Diplomas (PGDCA, PGDCA Advanced, DCA, DCP) | Programming for BTech (C, C++, Java, Oracle, Python) | Office & Accounts (Tally with GST, Office) | Non-coding. A quick "Which course is right for me?" picker by qualification (10th / Inter / Degree / BTech) routes to the right card.
5. **One detail page per course, Coursera order:** a fact strip (duration, eligibility, timings, certificate), what you'll learn, the software/tools, a module-by-module syllabus (accordion), a sample certificate image, faculty, related courses, FAQ, and a sticky "Enquire / WhatsApp" CTA. Title each page "<Course> course in Adoni" to win back the SEO-farm traffic.
6. **Upcoming batches block** (NareshIT), managed from the admin panel: course, start date, timing (morning 8-1 / evening 4-8), seats status "Admissions open". Admin-editable, so it never goes stale.
7. **Short enquiry form, 4 fields:** name, mobile (10-digit check), course (dropdown), preferred batch (morning/evening). Optional: qualification. Below it: "We reply on WhatsApp within working hours. No spam." On submit, a thank-you message plus a one-tap WhatsApp continue button. Save each enquiry in the admin panel with a status (new / called / joined).
8. **Certificate trust:** images of the ISO certificate, the AP DET permission letter and MSME registration, plus a sample course certificate. Add **"Verify a certificate"** (certificate number lookup from the admin panel), like Tally Education. Few or no local rivals offer this.
9. **Awards & Achievements as a dated timeline** (Jetking): year, award name, who issued it, photo. Plus a student achievers grid (name, course, year, photo), only with real data and consent. **[NEED: award names, years, issuers, photos; achiever list]**
10. **FAQ as objection handling:** eligibility per course, fees and instalments, certificate validity (ISO/govt permission), timings, lab practice per student, placement help (state it honestly), girls' safety and parents' visits, study material, BTech semester alignment, location and parking. Add FAQ schema for Google.

### Top 5 mistakes to avoid

1. **Dead or placeholder links** (`#` admissions, gallery, socials). This is the current site's biggest flaw and it appears in every local competitor too. Hide any section that has no real data.
2. **Fake or unbacked numbers:** no "100% placement", no "10,000+ students" or ratings unless RPC gives real records (Jetking's guarantee/disclaimer clash shows how it backfires). Mark them `[NEED: real figure]`.
3. **Hiding everything behind "call us":** no durations, no eligibility, no timings. Show at least duration, eligibility and timings for every course, and fees if the client agrees.
4. **Text walls and repeated sections** (Infotech, IT Wisdom). Keep short, scannable blocks with icons and one CTA per section.
5. **Inconsistent name/address/phone details** across the site, Google and directories. Use one exact name, address and phone everywhere. Add a Google Maps embed and LocalBusiness/EducationalOrganization schema.

### What would make RPC clearly the best in its region

- **The only institute in Adoni with a real, modern, mobile-first course website.** The bar locally is low (builder templates, SEO blogs, no forms). A clean site with real facts wins on its own.
- **Openness as the edge:** duration, eligibility, timings, a live batch calendar and (if the client agrees) fees on every course. Even the national brands hide most of this.
- **Proof no rival has:** 26 years (since 2000), government permission, ISO, awards, and online certificate verification, each backed by a document photo.
- **Local SEO built in:** one page per course with "in Adoni" in the title (beats the Thane SEO farm), Telugu-friendly copy (an English-first site with key lines also in Telugu, e.g. the hero subline and the CTA), FAQ schema, and a consistent Google Business Profile.
- **A WhatsApp-first enquiry flow** plus an admin panel that tracks every lead (source page, course, status), so RPC can follow up and see which courses pull enquiries.
- **Reviews:** after launch, ask past students for Google reviews, and link the real Google rating once it exists. Justdial rivals win on review counts, not on websites.

### Real data needed from the client (never invent)

Fees per course (or "ask on WhatsApp"), durations, eligibility, syllabus per course, batch dates, faculty names and photos, award details and photos, student achievers (with consent), testimonials (real, with consent), student count/placement numbers (only if records exist), certificate scans (ISO, DET 2006, MSME), the current address(es), lab/classroom photos, and the WhatsApp number to use.
